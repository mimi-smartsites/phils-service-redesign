      Welcome To Phil’s Service!

      We may be the new kid auto shop on the block but we’re not new to the complete auto center industry. You can count on our certified professionals for quality workmanship, personalized services, and competitive prices. We proudly service immediate auto service needs at our conveniently located auto shop with ten bays, state of the art equipment and a secure fenced-in area for car storage. No appointment necessary, we hear you coming!

      From oil changes to tire repairs, your one-stop auto shop offers quality services for all foreign and domestic vehicles. Our state of the art TechNet Professional and ASE certified technicians and GM Master Tech specialize in complete transmission and engine work, check engine light diagnostics, AC & heating services, front-end alignments as well as electrical, brake and suspension work. We also sell tires and are highly skilled in troubleshooting and preventative maintenance. For quality repairs and vehicle maintenance, contact us or stop by Phil’s Service today!

      Why Phil's

      Why Drivers & Fleet Managers Choose Phil’s Service

      As an ASE Blue Seal of Excellence facility, we hold our work to a higher standard focused on accurate diagnostics, verified repairs, and long-term vehicle performance.

      We take a diagnostic-first approach, identifying the root cause before any repairs begin. That means no guesswork and no unnecessary parts replacement. Every vehicle goes through a full digital inspection with photos and technician notes, so you can clearly see what we see.

      All auto repairs are backed by warranty and completed using factory procedures and OEM parts. Our goal is long-term reliability for your vehicle or fleet, helping reduce repeat issues and keep you on the road.
